PRIME: A systematic evolution perspective on intelligent posture-trajectory planning for robotic machining under multiple constraints

PRIME：多约束机器人加工智能姿态轨迹规划的系统演化视角

Juntong Su, Shengqiang Zhao, Fangyu Peng, Li Zhang, Xiaowei Tang, Xin Wang, Rong Yan, Hao Zhang

Publisher’s version↗Explore the benchmark↗

Abstract summary

PRIME reframes posture planning in robotic machining as a system-evolution problem in which posture changes along the toolpath inside a dynamic feasible region. Following a PRISMA-based review of 222 studies, the paper connects geometric, kinematic, mechanical, and process constraints, defines capability levels from redundancy-angle optimization to full-degree-of-freedom intelligent planning, and outlines a roadmap toward deployable systems.

Key contributions

  • A posture–trajectory evolution framework links constraint modeling, planning strategies, and system capability.
  • A unified formulation covers geometric, kinematic, mechanical, and process factors, with four levels of planning capability identified.
  • A public restricted-space milling benchmark supports comparisons between planners. The review also identifies open problems in modeling process constraints and validating planners in engineering settings.
