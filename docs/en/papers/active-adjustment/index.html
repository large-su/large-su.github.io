Robotics and Computer-Integrated Manufacturing · 2025 · Co-author

Extremely constrained robotic milling posture active adjustment on curved surface: Divide and conquer strategy with index of generalized accumulative work

极端约束下曲面机器人铣削姿态主动调整：基于广义累积功指标的分治策略

Shengqiang Zhao, Fangyu Peng, Juntong Su, Xiaowei Tang, Teng Zhang, Jiawei Wu, Rong Yan

Publisher’s version↗

Abstract summary

Curvature and feasible robot postures vary across a complex surface. The study combines robot and cutter–workpiece constraints in a generalized potential field, partitions the surface by curvature similarity, and uses a generalized accumulative-work measure to compare posture choices. Curved-surface milling experiments assess kinematic behavior and machining accuracy against commercial CAM planning.

Key contributions

  • A generalized potential field puts constraints on a common scale, and virtual potential torque drives posture adjustment.
  • Regions are divided by curvature similarity while maintaining high-order continuity across their boundaries.
  • Generalized accumulative work serves as a global measure for comparing feasible posture choices.
