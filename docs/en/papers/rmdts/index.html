Journal of Manufacturing Systems · 2025 · Co-author

RMDTs: Process-oriented function-triggered robotic milling digital twin system for service-expansion

RMDTs：面向加工过程与服务扩展的功能触发式机器人铣削数字孪生系统

Shengqiang Zhao, Fangyu Peng, Yunan Shan, Juntong Su, Xiaowei Tang, Rong Yan

Publisher’s version↗

Abstract summary

RMDTs presents a process-oriented digital-twin system for robotic milling of large and complex components. The system is organized around process context, function-triggered services, and extensibility. It connects intelligent planning with process monitoring and foreknowledge, and demonstrates information organization and service expansion across robotic machining cases with different workpieces, tools, and process conditions.

Key contributions

  • A robotic-milling digital-twin framework is built around process orientation, function triggering, and service expansion.
  • A dual-loop structure connects intelligent central planning with monitoring and foreknowledge.
  • Typical cases with different workpieces, tools, and process conditions examine the system's service extensibility.
