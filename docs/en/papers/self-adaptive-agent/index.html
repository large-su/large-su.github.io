Journal of Manufacturing Systems · 2024 · Co-author

A self-adaptive agent for flexible posture planning in robotic milling system

面向机器人铣削系统柔性姿态规划的自适应智能体

Shengqiang Zhao, Fangyu Peng, Juntong Su, Hao Sun, Rong Yan, Xiaowei Tang, Teng Zhang, Zepeng Li

Publisher’s version↗

Abstract summary

This paper addresses the changing geometric, kinematic, and accuracy constraints that arise during robotic milling. It proposes a self-adaptive agent for flexible posture planning, incorporates multiple constraint types into a unified model, and uses deep reinforcement learning to train posture policies that respond to time-varying machining conditions. Simulation and experiments are used for evaluation.

Key contributions

  • A flexible posture-planning model is proposed for time-varying robotic milling conditions.
  • Geometric, kinematic, and accuracy constraints are incorporated in one planning formulation.
  • Deep reinforcement learning trains and executes the self-adaptive posture agent, which is assessed in simulation and experiments.
