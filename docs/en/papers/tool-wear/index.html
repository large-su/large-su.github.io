Journal of Manufacturing Systems · 2026 · Co-author

Towards interpretable foreknowledge with multimodal information fusion: Physical-knowledge inspired time-varying tool wear intelligent prediction

融合多模态信息的可解释预知：物理知识驱动的时变刀具磨损预测

Shengqiang Zhao, Fangyu Peng, Xiuqing Hao, Ning He, Juntong Su, Ni Chen, Guolong Zhao, Yinfei Yang, Yunfeng Zhang, Hao Wang

Publisher’s version↗

Abstract summary

The study predicts how tool wear changes over time when training samples are limited. It aligns cutting-force, vibration, and acoustic-emission signals, incorporates physical knowledge of wear into the prediction model, and evaluates its performance and interpretability on titanium-alloy cutting data.

Key contributions

  • A physical-knowledge-inspired multimodal framework is proposed for time-varying tool-wear prediction.
  • Multi-cluster centralized prediction and Shapley superposition analysis improve interpretation across wear states.
  • The method is evaluated for scalability under few-shot, high-dimensional signals in difficult-to-machine titanium-alloy scenarios.
