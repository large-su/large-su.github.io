Sparse Representation of Robotic Machining Deformation Based on Key Points Determination

基于关键点确定的机器人加工变形稀疏表征

Yunan Shan, Shengqiang Zhao, Fangyu Peng, Rong Yan, Xiaowei Tang, Juntong Su, Hao Sun

Publisher’s version↗

Abstract summary

This paper addresses the difficulty of measuring process quantities such as cutting force and stiffness across a robotic machining task. It develops cutting-force and robot-end stiffness models, extracts ridge structures and key values from their distributions, and uses a small set of key points to reconstruct global deformation information. Sparse-representation error is used to assess the reliability of the reconstruction.

Key contributions

  • A key-point-based sparse representation strategy is proposed for robotic machining deformation.
  • PCA–GPR cutting-force and robot-end stiffness models provide the distributions from which key structures are extracted.
  • Representative locations on the distribution ridges and boundaries provide a compact description of the deformation across the part.
