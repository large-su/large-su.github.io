Investigation of the 3D-posture planning for space-restricted robotic milling: A potential network architecture

受限空间机器人铣削的三维姿态规划研究：一种势网络架构

Juntong Su, Shengqiang Zhao, Fangyu Peng, Xiaowei Tang, Rong Yan

Publisher’s version↗Explore the benchmark↗

Abstract summary

This paper addresses posture planning for robotic milling in restricted spaces. It introduces an extensible potential-network architecture that maps heterogeneous constraints and objectives, including accessibility, collision risk, and machining performance, into a common planning space. A posture manifold incorporates cutter–workpiece engagement so that orientations can evolve continuously along complex toolpaths. The approach is evaluated on a restricted-space benchmark and propeller machining tasks.

Key contributions

  • A modular planning architecture is established from potential units, potential functions, potential networks, and potential solutions for multi-constraint and multi-objective tasks.
  • A cutter–workpiece-aware posture manifold provides a parametric representation for three-dimensional orientation planning and rapid mode changes on complex surfaces.
  • Restricted-space benchmark tasks and representative propeller configurations are used to assess planning feasibility and adaptability.
