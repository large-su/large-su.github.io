Procedia CIRP · 2025 · Co-author

Discrete-superposition mechanism of multiple constraints for robotic machining posture twin planning

面向机器人加工姿态孪生规划的多约束离散叠加机制

Shengqiang Zhao, Fangyu Peng, Juntong Su, Xiaowei Tang, Rong Yan

Publisher’s version↗

Abstract summary

The study addresses robotic machining of large curved parts in restricted spaces. It examines geometric, kinematic, and performance constraints in layers and combines them discretely to represent feasible postures. Three machining tests on a large copper-alloy workpiece compare variation in cutting depth and surface roughness.

Key contributions

  • Constraints are examined in layers to determine how each narrows the feasible posture range.
  • The posture-twin planning model combines geometric feasibility with the effects of kinematic and machining performance constraints.
  • Copper-alloy milling trials evaluate machining performance when the constraints are considered together.
