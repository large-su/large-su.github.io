Robotic machining and motion planning

Juntong Su

苏俊通

School of Mechanical Science and Engineering, Huazhong University of Science and Technology

I study how industrial robots can machine complex surfaces in confined spaces. My work focuses on planning continuous tool orientations along a prescribed toolpath under geometric, kinematic, and machining constraints.

Selected papers↓ GitHub↗

Research

  • Restricted-space robotic milling

    Finding reachable tool orientations for parts such as propellers, where obstacles, the workpiece, and robot geometry limit access.

  • Constraint-aware posture and trajectory planning

    Planning continuous machining postures along a toolpath while accounting for geometric, kinematic, mechanical, and process constraints.

  • Machining performance and validation

    Using benchmarks and machining experiments to evaluate planning outcomes, with related work on deformation, errors, and tool wear.

Investigation of the 3D-posture planning for space-restricted robotic milling: A potential network architecture

受限空间机器人铣削的三维姿态规划研究：一种势网络架构

A potential network brings planning constraints and objectives into one model. A posture manifold accounts for cutter–workpiece engagement. The approach was evaluated on a restricted-space benchmark and propeller milling tasks.

PRIME: A systematic evolution perspective on intelligent posture-trajectory planning for robotic machining under multiple constraints

PRIME：多约束机器人加工智能姿态轨迹规划的系统演化视角

This review of 222 studies treats machining posture as a planning variable that evolves under constraints along the toolpath. It identifies four capability levels, from redundancy-angle optimization to full-degree-of-freedom planning.

Juntong Su, Shengqiang Zhao, Fangyu Peng, Li Zhang, Xiaowei Tang, Xin Wang, Rong Yan, Hao Zhang
